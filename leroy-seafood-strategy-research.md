# Leroy Seafood Group: 2026-2030 Strategy Research

## Four Strategic Pillars: Growth - Cost - Simplify - Leadership

> **Note:** Leroy Seafood Group ASA is hosting its Capital Markets Days on **March 2-3, 2026** in Bergen, Norway, where the formal 2030 strategy and these four pillars will be officially presented. The research below synthesizes publicly available information as of February 10, 2026.

---

## Company Overview

- **Full Name:** Leroy Seafood Group ASA (OB: LSG)
- **Founded:** 1899, Bergen, Norway
- **CEO:** Henning Beltestad (since March 2010)
- **Employees:** ~6,000 across 17 countries
- **Markets:** 80+ countries globally
- **2024 Revenue:** ~NOK 31 billion
- **2030 Revenue Target:** NOK 50 billion (~USD 4.8B / EUR 4.9B)
- **Historical Growth:** 12% average annual revenue growth over 20 years
- **ROCE Target:** 18%

### Business Segments
1. **Farming** - Salmon and trout production across three Norwegian regions (Leroy Sjotroll, Leroy Midt, Leroy Aurora) + 50% ownership of Scottish Sea Farms. ~1,800 employees.
2. **VAPS&D (Value-Added Processing, Sales & Distribution)** - Downstream processing, global sales, and distribution. ~3,200 employees across 14 countries.
3. **Wild Catch** - 10 trawlers and 10 processing plants in Northern Norway. ~1,000 employees.

---

## Pillar 1: GROWTH

### Revenue Ambition
- **Target:** NOK 50 billion annual revenue by 2030, up from ~NOK 31 billion in 2024.
- **Historical Context:** NOK 26.6B (2022 record), NOK 30B+ (2023, first time), maintaining ~12% CAGR over the last two decades.

### Harvest Volume Growth
- **2024 Actual:** 171,200 GWT (Norway only)
- **2025 Actual:** 195,600 GWT (Norway only)
- **2026 Expected:** 195,000 GWT (Norway) / 217,500 GWT (including Scottish Sea Farms share)
- The group is investing in biology improvements to drive sustainable volume increases.

### Geographic Expansion (Project Dragonbite)
A strategic initiative to build presence in high-growth Asian markets:
- **2025:** Opened offices in Thailand, Vietnam, Korea, and India
- **Thailand:** Serves as a regional hub for Southeast Asia
- **India:** Leveraging the EFTA free trade agreement that significantly reduced salmon/trout tariffs
- **Strategic Customer Target:** 80% of revenue from strategic customers by 2030

### Organic Growth & Technology
- **Aquatraz C2 (Joint venture with SalMar):** Six fully closed containment units, each 70,000m3 water volume with water intakes at 35m depth. First fish planned Q1 2027, full operation from 2028. This will double floating closed containment production capacity in Norway.
- **Scottish Sea Farms:** 50/50 JV with SalMar. Acquired Grieg Seafood Hjaltland UK Ltd. in 2021 to expand Scottish operations. Expected to contribute 22,500 GWT in 2026.

### VAPS&D Growth
- **EBIT Target:** NOK 1.25 billion (set in 2022) - effectively achieved with rolling 12-month EBIT reaching NOK 1,249M as of Q3 2025.
- Record segment earnings driven by increased plant utilization and reduced raw material costs.
- Strong growth in Asia with expanding market demand.

## Pillar 2: COST

### Farming Cost Optimization
- **2026 Outlook:** Cost per harvested kg expected to be lower in 2026 than 2025, primarily driven by lower feed costs.
- **Farming Segment Target:** Production cost reduction of NOK 4.6/kg (in 2021 money) was set as a 2025 target.
- **Challenge Context:** Q3 2025 saw operational EBIT of NOK -306M in Farming due to salmon spot prices being NOK 8/kg lower YoY combined with higher costs from high sea temperatures and sea lice pressure.

### Feed Innovation & Cost Reduction
- Developing alternative feed ingredients with lower cost and carbon footprint:
  - Microalgae, kelp, insect meal (1.5-2% in freshwater feeds), mussel meal
  - Poultry by-products as a large-scale, cost-effective protein source
- Quarterly meetings with feed suppliers to drive innovation and cost improvements.

### Operational Efficiency
- **Shore Power/Electrification:** 80%+ of sites now run on shore power, reducing energy costs. Hybrid solutions for remaining sites achieve 30% better fuel efficiency.
- **Integrated Value Chain Advantage:** CEO Henning Beltestad describes the fully integrated model as "more reliable, faster and cost efficient," enabling full traceability, quality assurance, and value creation through product innovation.
- **VAPS&D Cost Performance:** Record EBIT driven by increased plant utilization and optimized raw material sourcing from own upstream operations.

### Market Context
- A more balanced salmon market expected in 2026 following stabilization of record-high supply growth in 2025.
- Wild Catch: Reduced cod quotas in 2026 offset by record-high cod prices. Quotas expected to increase again from 2027.

## Pillar 3: SIMPLIFY

### The Leroy Way (Business System)
- **Launched:** 2017, after a record-results year.
- **Philosophy:** "One Leroy" - unified approach across the entire value chain.
- **Purpose:** Provides guiding principles for how to operate reliably while continuously improving and creating long-term value.
- **Led by:** Jonas Langeteig (Head of Leroy Way Office)
- **Adoption:** By 2025, everyone in Leroy was expected to have adopted the Leroy Way. Scoring trends for adoption have been positive, increasing every year.

### Integrated Value Chain Simplification
- Leroy's fully integrated model (from raw material to end product) inherently simplifies operations by:
  - Removing intermediaries between farming, processing, and distribution
  - Enabling direct traceability from ocean to table
  - Streamlining product development cycles with direct customer feedback loops
  - Coordinating supply chain decisions across segments

### Automation & Digital Transformation
- Strategic emphasis on automation to reduce complexity and improve reliability.
- Investments in new technology at processing facilities.
- Microsoft Power Platform deployed for improved reporting, reportedly reducing costs by millions of dollars.
- Knowledge campaign (June 2025) on transparency in operations, demonstrating commitment to clear communication.

### Operational Standardization
- Continuous improvement philosophy embedded across all operations.
- Standardized processes through the Leroy Way framework.
- Shared expertise and synergies across regions (Sjotroll, Midt, Aurora).
- The scoring/audit system for Leroy Way adoption ensures consistent execution.

## Pillar 4: LEADERSHIP

### Industry Leadership
- **#1 Target:** Become the industry leader in EBIT/kg for both Farming and VAPS&D.
- **Sustainability Recognition:**
  - Ranked #2 (previously #1) on the Coller FAIRR Protein Producer Index (top 60 global protein producers).
  - Named climate champion by PwC's annual climate index for two consecutive years.
  - One of only nine Norwegian companies aligned with the Paris Agreement.
- **Market Position:** One of the world's largest producers of Atlantic salmon, and a leading wild fish company in Norway.

### Sustainability & ESG Leadership
- **GHG Target:** 46% reduction in absolute Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions by 2030 (2019 baseline), approved by the Science Based Targets Initiative (SBTi), aligned with 1.5C pathway.
  - Scope 3 emissions (~85% of total) at 1,021,417 tCO2e are included in the target.
- **Gender Diversity:** Target of 40% women in leadership positions by 2030.
- **Memberships:** UN Global Compact, Global Sustainable Seafood Initiative, Amnesty International.
- **Reporting:** CSRD-compliant double materiality assessment, GRI Standards 2021, ESEF machine-readable format.

### Management & Governance
- **CEO:** Henning Beltestad - 15+ year tenure, providing strategic continuity.
- **Average Management Tenure:** 7 years.
- **Average Board Tenure:** 3.1 years.
- **Board Oversight:** Board directly oversees sustainability and ESG issues, including strategic oversight, risk management, and climate targets.
- **Head of ESG & Quality:** Anne Hilde Midttveit coordinates sustainability across the Group.

### Innovation Leadership
- **Aquatraz C2:** Pioneering next-generation closed containment aquaculture.
- **Feed Innovation:** Early adopter of alternative proteins (insect meal, poultry by-products) at scale.
- **New Aquaculture Showcase Center:** Opened in Kirkenes (2025) for community engagement and transparency.
- **Knowledge Campaign:** Comprehensive public education initiative on modern aquaculture practices.

### People & Culture
- "One Leroy" culture driven by the Leroy Way business system.
- Employee engagement through continuous improvement frameworks.
- 6,000 employees delivering ~5 million seafood meals daily.
